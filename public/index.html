About TrackGimex

Built for Kenyan rentals

TrackGimex is a simple rent tracker for people who own or manage rental houses: bedsitters, one and two bedrooms, apartments and plots. Amounts are in Kenyan shillings and payments support M-Pesa, so landlords, caretakers, agents and tenants work from the same up-to-date record.
