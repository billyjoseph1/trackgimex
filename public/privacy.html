Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data TrackGimex collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 (the "Act"). It applies to landlords, agents, caretakers and tenants who use TrackGimex at trackgimex.netlify.app and any other address we use for the service.

1. Who we are

TrackGimex is operated by TrackGimex, Nairobi, Kenya ("TrackGimex", "we", "us").

Contact for privacy questions and requests: trackgimexapp@gmail.com.

2. Our role: controller and processor

  • Your own account (your name, email, phone, password, settings): TrackGimex is the data controller.
  • Tenant information that a landlord or agent enters (for example a tenant added by name and phone number, their rent records and receipts): the landlord or agent is the data controller and TrackGimex processes that data on their behalf as a data processor. Landlords and agents are responsible for having a lawful basis to record their tenants' details and for telling their tenants about it.

4. Lawful basis

We process personal data under section 30 of the Act because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the service you signed up for), to comply with legal obligations (for example keeping payment records), for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, and, where needed, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Money and payments

TrackGimex never receives or holds rent or deposits. Tenants pay landlords directly through M-Pesa (to the landlord's own Paybill or Till) or by bank transfer. When you use an M-Pesa prompt, Safaricom processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy and tells us the result so we can record it.

6. Who we share data with

  • Between landlords and their tenants: a landlord sees the details and payments of tenants in their houses and of people who request their houses; a tenant sees their own house, payments and their landlord's contact details. Other users cannot see your data.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • Supabase — database, login and server functions (data stored in the European Union, Ireland)
    • Netlify — website hosting
    • Brevo — sending account emails (confirmations, password resets)
    • Safaricom PLC — M-Pesa payments, when a landlord has connected M-Pesa
    • jsDelivr and Google Fonts — deliver website code and fonts (they see your IP address)
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

7. Transfers outside Kenya

Our database is hosted in the European Union (Ireland), and some providers operate from other countries. We only use providers that apply appropriate safeguards for personal data (including encryption and contractual data-protection terms), as required by sections 48–50 of the Act.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data: while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account we delete or anonymise it within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.
  • Payment and tenancy records: kept for 5 years after the record was created, because landlords and tenants rely on them for tax and dispute purposes.
  • A landlord's M-Pesa keys: until the landlord disconnects M-Pesa or deletes their account, then deleted immediately.
  • Technical logs: a short period (typically up to 90 days) set by our hosting providers.

9. How we protect data

All traffic uses HTTPS. Database access rules make sure each user can only reach their own data. Passwords are hashed; M-Pesa keys are encrypted. Access to the systems is limited to the people who run TrackGimex. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and, where required, you, as set out in section 43 of the Act.

10. Your rights

Under section 26 of the Act you have the right to:

  • be informed of how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct false or misleading data (you can edit most of it in your profile);
  • delete data that is false, misleading or no longer needed;
  • object to processing of all or part of your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format (landlords can export payments as CSV).

To use any of these rights, email trackgimexapp@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will reply within 7 working days where possible and in any case within the time the law allows. If your request is about tenant information a landlord entered, we may pass it to that landlord, who is responsible for it.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC): www.odpc.go.ke.

11. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a login session so you stay logged in; logging out removes it.

12. Children

TrackGimex is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you by email or in the app.
